# Dark-Sky Field Notes

Field guide for astronomy-club members planning dark-sky stargazing trips in US national parks.

**Start here:** [field_notes.md](field_notes.md) — the full guide, containing:

- **How we searched** — the three arXiv search angles, the exact queries, and how results were pooled and deduplicated
- **Paper shortlist** — 12 arXiv preprints on night-sky brightness and light pollution (sky-brightness measurement methods, wildlife/ecosystem effects, satellite remote sensing of artificial light at night), with arXiv ids
- **Four dark-sky parks** — Acadia, Big Bend, Death Valley, Great Basin: the visitor centers where members pick up maps and ask about astronomy programs, plus current NPS alerts relevant to night visits, exactly as the park service returned them
- **Image picks** — 5 representative night-sky photo URLs covering all four parks, after near-duplicate removal
- **Computed counts** — raw vs. unique paper numbers, visitor centers per park, alerts, images kept — all tallied by script, not by hand

Compiled 2026-10-19 from arXiv searches, the National Park Service API, and web image search.
